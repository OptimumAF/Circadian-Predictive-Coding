Why this: the full paired readout remains incomplete at its original240s limit.
Existing R2.1/return probes already provide the diagnostic; quotas lacked the
owned-snapshot integration. A full snapshot bridge preserves parameters,
gradients, momentum and native counters in an independently owned array learner.

protect the original candidate, source/actor, receipts, quotas, sampling state,
budgets and logical clock. The initial probe's success is checked before further
TRAIN work and throughout the end probe. Final runtime readback is guarded too.

The initial→end contrast includes BOTH the prefix and reward history. It cannot
attribute changes to reward training alone. E is a deterministic reserved task;
separate record identities do not make this a generalization result. This route
does not establish old-task retention/recovery, trained matched BP/PC/CPC controls
or a circadian advantage. Those parent requirements remain open.
