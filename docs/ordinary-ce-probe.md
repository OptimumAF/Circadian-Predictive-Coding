Python: `run_example(context_return=True, method="bp", reserved_probe=True,
probe_initialization="ce")`. Omit `probe_initialization` to keep the existing
reserved-probe behavior for BP, PC, neutral CPC and active CPC. The explicit
initialization rejects other families, non-exact strings and conflicting modes
before model/data construction. It is not a new circadian algorithm or baseline
retuning based on final evaluation.

At the original source12 handoff, `scripts/ticket_ce_probe.py` composes the
existing source-admission predicate, TRAIN-only float32 CE fitter, detached
initializer and independent private probe. No per-experiment monkeypatch or
scalar-checkpoint loader is needed. Actual source state/optimizer/RNG are pinned;
source budget, actor and prior history retain the runner's original authority.
Authoritative runtime/inbox/ledger/receipt objects are pinned by identity and
value. Public receipt views are deep copies and are compared by value. Mutation
or replacement is refused and preserved, not repaired by restoring evidence.

After one fit on the original old-TRAIN12 rows, only detached output coefficients
change. Original source native counters, policy, full SGD momentum and gradient
buffers remain intact. Why: resetting momentum or rescaling the large fitted
logits would change the intended continuation. Auxiliary CE work is separately
paid; its joint old-TRAIN reuse is rehearsal, even when the stream replay counter
is zero. Neither source nor deployed actor receives fitted or probe parameters.

The unchanged probe uses16fresh-task updates48rows on v1parcel/v2password,
old4 TRAIN monitoring only during F, then16old-TRAIN return updates96rows on the
same detached learner. Probe-test labels only score; final evaluation is absent
from fitting, probing, selection and promotion. All six tasks are evaluated at
the existing three phases with original allowed actions/max6steps/success rules.
This describes known templates, not unseen generalization or statistical benefit.

Budgets stay distinct: original source12/30s/512MiB with original birth; auxiliary
15s/512MiB from before TRAIN preparation through initialization; existing F and
return each16/30s/512MiB, born once together. Original source time continues
through auxiliary/probe work; return's clock includes F. Auxiliary completed
time does not restart during the private phase. The same prepared authority and
paid-row guards remain active across handoff and private callbacks, including
immediately after the original source clock and before more private work.

Reports keep these separate:

- Root `training_work`, `budget`, `costs`: original source only.
- `probe_initialization`: complete/error, fit logits/work, full initialization
  definition/continuation/coefficients/losses/work, preserved paid work and the
  original auxiliary session. A complete initialization is not task success.
- `reserved_probe`: original private update/cost/curve/task/session records.

The bounded4MiB writer includes auxiliary paid evidence in its scalar-cost
fallback, even if fitting fails before private work begins. Paid rows cannot be
reused to reset charges. Operational failures stop later admission. Native update
counts exclude auxiliary optimizer steps; equal native counts are not equal
computation or total row exposure.
