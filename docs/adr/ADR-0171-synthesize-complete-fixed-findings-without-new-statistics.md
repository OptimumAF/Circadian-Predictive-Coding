# ADR-0171: Synthesize complete fixed findings without new statistics

## Context

The accepted primary, development, activity, outcome/cost and matrix ledgers
have distinct roles and measurement scopes. Raw cost contexts retain skipped
and rejected proposals. The first outcome/cost publication timed out with a
claim still present; its terminal child counters are unobserved. Successful
later operations do not erase that failed attempt. Joining tables without
these limits could turn development selection, extra exposure or inactivity
into an unsupported mechanism claim.

## Decision

Bind six whole canonical bodies and their repeats, plus complete relevant
handoffs, failed partial parts and exact source snapshots in a fixed catalog.
Separate pure input validation, operational failure interpretation, findings
and rendering. Rebuild existing complete primary/development/activity/matrix
declarations; join every original outcome/cost cell. Preserve all original raw
inputs and literal UTF-8 records in the companion JSON. Exhaustive Markdown
keeps every cell/pair/statement/decision and complete indexes to those records.

Why this: complete indexes keep the readable report usable while retaining
all raw states/settings/roles/metrics in one self-contained result. Repeating
hundreds of megabytes of raw states in Markdown adds no interpretation.
H1–H4 remain unresolved in these fixed measured settings; original primary
simultaneous intervals and eligibility are unchanged. No new statistic,
selection, family pooling, vote, winner or mechanistic claim is introduced.

## Alternatives

A selected score table or final-counter-only report loses rejected/skipped
activity and raw negative/null evidence. Replacing original simultaneous
intervals with marginal or selected intervals changes the analysis contract.
A completed nonterminal audit cannot prove a claim-held publication completed.

## Consequences

All complete sources/inputs are frozen before behavioral fixtures. The already
declared 180-second pure envelope bounds each whole stored-input derivation.
The pure result grants no fresh current official-reader authority. P6.12b3
must separately verify current inputs/sources and execute two publications and
two independent complete readbacks under prospectively justified budgets.
All original scientific and reader gates and parent criteria remain unchanged.
