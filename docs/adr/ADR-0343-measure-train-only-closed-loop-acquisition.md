# ADR-0343: Measure train-only closed-loop acquisition

## Fixed A exposure extension, 2026-10-16

The explicit A128 mean control fills the documented missing equal-A-exposure
comparison to A64/B64/A64 (128A+64B). The same helper accepts only64(default) or
128 as its endpoint, validating original completed/max budget and fixed owned
states/slots accordingly. Why: share the actual legal train-only readout without
a generic evaluator or new runner. Default64 behavior/refusal rules remain.
Raw historical return comparison is separate from standalone legal0/128 live;
old unmasked live results are not a matched action-law comparison. Native work
128vs192 and initial incomplete acquisition forbid recovery-savings claims.

## Context

The existing64-update A± stream measures teacher-label fitting. Accepted reward
training has produced zero returns; useful acquisition is still unestablished.
Final evaluation cannot guide training or diagnostic selection. The unchanged
pooled train-label prior may have zero mass on an encountered legal support.

## Decision

Add an explicit opt-in readout after all64 commits, through the existing stream
and evaluator. Owned0/64 forks play both fixed training cues; the pooled prior
plays both once with the same legal information. Keep all original fit points,
budgets, receipts, clocks and permanent charges. Bind six bounded episode slots
and preceding evidence before admitting more work.

Only independently proven actual zero legal mass permits an explicit prior
refusal and continuation of the other planned cue. Preserve applied prefix work
and pending unexecuted decision; no completed prior metric or numeric comparison
exists unless both reference episodes complete. All other faults stop work.

## Alternatives

Teacher fitting alone cannot answer whether a policy completes its training task.
Final-driven selection violates evaluation isolation. Smoothing the prior changes
the frozen reference; scoring refusal as failure invents a completed outcome.
A separate runner duplicates existing authority and accounting boundaries.

## Consequences

No new mathematical objective or dependency is introduced. The accepted native
case shows improved teacher fit but zero live acquisition; its prior comparison
is undefined. The diagnostic has explicit additional forward/physics costs and
establishes no generalization, circadian advantage or release readiness.
