## Context

The accepted initialization diagnostic found a large B input-scale/saturation
shift under frozen A-only statistics. It cannot answer whether actual training
learns B, retains A or recovers when A returns. Native fresh guards previously
rejected the larger nearest candidate; those results remain evaluation-only.

## Decision

Add one focused adapter composing existing continuous MLP, diagnostics and
ActorShadowRuntime, exposed as an explicit mode in the current stream CLI.
Use default policy and fixed A8/B8/A8, one A-only fit and one 24-update budget.
Read both contexts at all fixed endpoints, retain all losses/signed metrics and
costs, and keep source/actor unchanged. Validate preceding receipt/state/charge
and final empty-poll state. Preserve bounded cumulative receipts on failure.

## Alternatives

Another runner or publication/checkpoint framework does not address learning.
Automatically changing normalization, optimizer or epochs in response to the
conditioning readout would confound this diagnostic sequence. Guard/probe/final
labels cannot be used for fitting or selecting a favorable recipe.

## Consequences

The result measures same-task TRAIN imitation on saved reset contexts, with
explicit repeated exposure and diagnostic overhead. It cannot establish physical
skill, generalization, causal conditioning benefit or circadian advantage. Future
comparisons require separate frozen protocols. Original matched baselines and
accepted R2.1/checkpoint evidence are reused; full R4.4/R4.5 remain open.
