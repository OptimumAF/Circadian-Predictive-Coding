# ADR-0170: Preserve original transactions with their control scope

## Context

Final method counters do not retain every proposal, skipped policy or rejection
reason. The accepted raw cost inspection contains these records. Replay pilots
have shared offered identities and attempt counters, without individual sleep
commit records. Schedule has one neutral controller and three matched appliers.
Combined/parent factors retain individual transactions and selector state.

## Decision

Add a pure activity projection as a tested prerequisite of the complete b2
findings. Bind both whole original cost inspection and outcome/cost bodies;
preserve all 3,650 guarded/skipped decision records and sixty shared replay
offers, their original outcomes/reasons/guards/proposals/selector state and
source pointers. Join all 560 cells and every original scoped resource field.
Check original guard signs, commitment/rollback rules and attempt counts.

Keep schedule controller transactions separate from their matched appliers.
Keep unrecorded replay commits unmeasured. Do not infer zero commits from an
absent record or a model name. Empty proposals, zero attempts and skipped
policies remain evidence; they do not establish a beneficial mechanism.

The already declared 180-second pure saved-metadata envelope covers the much
larger complete raw contexts. Freeze all inputs/sources before fixtures and
require two bounded actual complete derivations. Scientific and original
current-reader gates remain intact.

## Alternatives

- Infer activity from final counters: loses rollback work/reasons and mistakes
  controller followers for independent transactions.
- Re-run models to collect new telemetry: changes the measured scope.
- Treat unrecorded commits as zero: invents an observation.

## Consequences

The projection retains complete raw records and can be large. It computes no
new scientific metric, interval, winner or hypothesis decision. b2 must still
combine these facts with all development/confirmation evidence and operational
failures. b3 must prove complete current publication/readbacks.
