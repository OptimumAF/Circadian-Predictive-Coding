# ADR-0179: Bind the complete prospective design to unresolved saved evidence

## Context

The original six-family matched/informative study and complete original reader
witnesses are saved. Historical ten-seed results and deterministic repeats do
not establish fresh roles, actual release UTC, independent source replications
or prospective precision. The original semantic resource repeat failed.

## Decision

Add a pure ordered replication contract, complete design/evidence consumers and
a separate whole-file boundary. Preserve all configurations, cells, contrasts,
480 role requirements, eight offsets, original analysis/stopping rules and caps.
Keep actual source/seed/role/request identities unset until independently bound.
Retain all 4,471 content witnesses, 13,149 aliases, unresolved effects, both full
original-reader outputs and all 116 original precision vectors. Reuse whole
previously verified outputs with complete actual source/input/proof preservation;
record no new historical reader execution in this consumer.

Why this: a pending structural contract is testable before any source or final
role exists, and prevents future code from filling missing provenance by
interpreting distinct integers, copied audits or synthetic fixture flags as proof.

## Alternatives

Narrow header/early-record checks omit late uncertainty and fixed scope. Repeating
original readers establishes the same historical witnesses and does not resolve
new roles. Selecting seeds or launching confirmation now bypasses outstanding
prior-use, resource, isolation and reproducibility criteria. None is adopted.

## Consequences

The structural component can be accepted after its complete fixtures, static
checks, two deterministic saved projections and preservation gate. Original
P6.7/b2/b3 parents retain all criteria and remain unfinished. Actual independent
source/role/request bindings, b3 proofs and explicit failed-resource resolution
remain next work. Negative precision results and historical failures remain valid;
no baseline, metric, scientific cap, seed or production matcher changes.
