# ADR-0586: Diagnose revised policy fit from saved native evidence

## Context

The frozen five-controller study produced identical task outcomes: revised v1
invoice failed even after four updates. A controller sweep cannot explain the
learner gap. The existing NumPy diagnostic already binds TRAIN fit to paid native
scores, but its unqualified three-row clocks and v2 teacher cannot admit these
qualified96/576 plus six-row-update states.

## Decision

Add one explicit mode to that reader/CLI. Preserve old admissions and mathematical
routines. Admit the complete original study, common source and ALL opportunity4
states, fixed doses, v1 TRAIN/public alignment and full finite float32 heads.
Normalize only the exact JSON clock mapping needed by the existing outcome
validator. Validate ALL54paid decisions/270scores with unchanged1e-6 tolerance
before interpreting any six-row fit. CLI pins original bytes and reserves fresh
output. No learner, restore/update, probe, final read or new framework is needed.

Why this: saved fit separates an observed wrong action from average-loss trends
without purchasing another comparative model cohort or changing its outcomes.

## Alternatives and consequences

Broadly relax old source clocks: rejected; the original recipe stays strict.
Re-run native readouts: unnecessary; already-paid raw scores suffice for parity.
Increase dose or select a favorable checkpoint: not part of this diagnosis.
Infer loss of plasticity from a negative four-update task result: unsupported;
an isolated warm-versus-fresh probe is the next causal learning question.

The fixed case fits5/6rows throughout. Revised invoice route−inspect margin stays
near−3.64 while mean loss falls. This preserves the task negative and establishes
no controller/CPC benefit. Focused41checks/platform and scoped static checks pass;
test typing/no-op-fixture failures and a prospective software allowance revision
remain preserved. Source/procedure/results are self-reviewed, not independent
acceptance. See issue70 and artifacts/runs/policy-train-fit-20261023/RESULTS.md.
