# ADR-0510: Private TRAIN velocity fit control

## Context

Saved Push diagnostics showed known-input error before old correction coverage
departed. The accepted fixed balanced fit retained inherited optimizer velocity.
The existing paired zero-velocity control admits reserved probe roles; released
TRAIN data must not be relabeled to reuse it.

## Decision

Share the original four-array zero operation in continuous_probe_control, with a
separate exact-native TRAIN private preparation contract. Check both owners,
arrays and states around every callback/getter, restore copied expected state,
preserve source parameters/normalization/counters/policy, and retain paid failures.
Compose this helper with existing study reconstruction, weighted eight updates
and private five-group readouts. Compare one frozen same-source/data/weights/dose
case against the accepted inherited control. No physical scene or candidate release.

## Alternatives

Changing probe roles would violate isolation. A new optimizer or runner adds no
needed capability. A seed/weight/dose search would change the scientific question.

## Consequences

273 checks per platform, scoped statics and independent actual acceptance pass.
Correction mean fit improves slightly; first/expert and all old-group means worsen.
No broad repair, causal explanation, physical success or CPC advantage follows.
Reconstruction costs are paid again because accepted reports do not contain
loadable model arrays. Full costs and negative results remain preserved.
