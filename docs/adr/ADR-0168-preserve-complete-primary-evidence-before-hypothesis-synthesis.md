Keep pure application logic and rendering separate from IO/current provenance.
Existing 127 scientific/reader sources remain unchanged. New code has no source,

deterministic rendering and whole-input/output checks, before those fixtures.
Prior scientific budgets and reader caps remain unchanged. Repeat all stored
inputs; a deterministic repeat adds no independent seed replication.

## Alternatives

- Summarize a favorable family or marginal interval: changes the prospective
  simultaneous scope and leaves required null/negative evidence behind.
- Decide each broad hypothesis from a majority of primary signs: an undeclared
  aggregation that does not establish a capacity, exposure or compute tradeoff.
- Combine all IO and interpretation at once: obscures a small pure correctness
  boundary and makes later failure/provenance omissions harder to detect.

## Consequences

P6.12a cannot close P6.12 or any broader confirmation/scientific parent. The
next increment must retain every development/tuning, sleep and operational
failure fact, then prove complete current bindings and independent readbacks.
No scientific settings, baselines, seeds, metrics, interval assumptions,
dependencies or occupied historical artifacts change.
