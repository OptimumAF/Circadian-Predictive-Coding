# ADR-0637: evaluate the frozen affine endpoint on final D

Status: accepted for the explicit final-only binding and one fixed observation.

## Context

The ordinary affine learner already acquires G, retains ABC and plays known G/A
from its saved actual endpoint. Held-out-layout behavior is a separate roadmap
requirement. Existing final-D admission accepts only FIFO36 benchmark128, and
must retain that narrower contract. Neural125 lost its paid endpoint; it cannot
be refitted or reconstructed within the spent allowance.

## Decision

Add one explicit final-only binding for original affine10/252/head36 data and
native0source, delegating to the existing game loop and full snapshot/budget
guards. Use only original Dblue/red, fixed role/seed/horizon/all7firstargmax, no
fit or target scoring. Reuse the accepted saved coefficients and existing
dataclass converter, not a new loader or trainer. Keep all historical costs,
failures, protocol identities and final/train separation.

## Alternatives

Widening FIFO or TRAIN admission would obscure different model and data-role
contracts. Repeating neural training would evade a spent study. Another known
task or checkpoint readout would not answer the missing held-out criterion.
No new architecture or objective is needed for evaluation of an already saved
working control.

## Consequences

The fixed result is1/2success: blue truncates at16/return0; red terminates at13/
return.26875. Model/source state, both environments, RNG and trajectories are checked.
No policy changes follow these final outcomes. D is held out from this model's
TRAIN but globally seen; one public layout and seed do not establish statistical
generalization, reliability, causal/equal-compute or circadian benefit. Parent
R4.2 recurrent RL and broader science/release criteria remain open. The binding
does not repair historical missing neural/private outcomes.
