# ADR-0172: Rebuild current findings through complete reader ports

## Context

P6.12b2 preserves the entire fixed evidence and exhaustive findings. Its saved
input derivations cannot establish fresh current reader authority. P6.12b3 must
independently validate current outcome/cost, matrix and development inputs for
each publication and readback. The first P6.10 timeout remains incomplete and
its killed child's terminal counters remain unobserved.

## Decision

Add three explicit complete reader ports in `app`, composed by the fixed CLI.
The outcome/cost and matrix ports each dispatch their unchanged complete reader
and its separate unchanged complete original report reader. The development
port invokes all original development validators. Every operation calls all
three ports again, then uses the unchanged public pure synthesis and rendering.
The entire result and Markdown must equal the accepted b2 bytes.

Bind all 144 accepted sources, the six new source/catalog files, all accepted
and new tests, complete b1/b2 physical inputs, all four current upstream bundles,
handoffs, environment and preserved failures. Verify complete bindings before
readers, after readers and at final publication/readback. Own output through an
exclusive unique claim; preserve foreign claims/audits and all failed parts.
Readbacks reconstruct the whole result, Markdown and audit from fresh ports.

Declare an 840-second outer envelope before fixtures: unchanged 240-second
outcome/cost, 180-second matrix, 120-second development and 180-second pure
synthesis/presentation ceilings total 720 seconds, plus 120 seconds for current
bindings, ownership and artifact checks. Each actual child gets a hard outer
cap. Two publications and two independent readbacks have a 3,360-second maximum
operation budget. Original report/scientific caps, settings and source bytes
remain unchanged. A timeout is evidence of failure within this declared budget.

Why this: independent full dispatches retain each existing authority boundary.
Stable whole-part identities in the audit make readback reconstruction possible
without treating a new validation duration as a repeat of historical timing.
Current validation durations belong in separate operation traces. Large saved
raw contexts load only after the two report readers return, keeping their
unchanged resource gates independent of derivative presentation allocations.

## Alternatives

Reusing a completed report between ports or operations would omit an original
reader dispatch. Narrowing the readers or projecting a subset would weaken
authority and preservation. Changing accepted pure sources would reopen b2.
None satisfies the current acceptance contract.

## Consequences

The result remains large and each operation revalidates two complete reports.
No new training, scoring, source access, tuning, metric, interval, seed,
dependency or experiment is introduced. Original H1–H4 uncertainty, negative
and null observations, rollback work, activity/inactivity, scoped costs and
failed history remain. Publication alone does not close b3/b/P6.12; original
acceptance audits and actual repeated fresh reader evidence are still required.
